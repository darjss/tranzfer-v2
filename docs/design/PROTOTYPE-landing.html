How it works

Three steps. Zero babysitting.

01

Drop the cards.

Whole camera cards, folders, 400 GB. Bytes go from your browser straight to storage, never through our servers.

02

Walk away.

Real speed, real ETA. Every chunk is checkpointed. Whatever happens to your connection, nothing already sent is sent twice.

03

Send one link.

Your editor clicks and downloads. No account. The link expires when the job is done and the files delete themselves.
