We make Tranzfer, so weigh our ranking with that in mind. Smash is good value and for small sends it's hard to beat. There's a section below on when to stay. Every fact about another service links to the page it came from.

## Why do people look for a Smash alternative?

**The free queue.** Smash Free has no hard size limit, which sounds great. The catch is in the footnote on the [pricing page](https://fromsmash.com/pricing): "Beyond 2 GB, transfers join a queue behind Pro transfers." Their [size limit help page](https://fromsmash.com/help/articles/13885610-what-is-the-file-size-limit-on-smash) says the same thing, that free transfers over 2 GB "are not prioritized and are placed in a waiting queue." Neither says how long the wait is.

**Starting over.** This is the bigger one for large files. Smash's help page on [uploads stuck at 100%](https://fromsmash.com/help/articles/12985104-my-transfer-gets-stuck-at-100) is blunt about it: "Unfortunately, there is no way to restart the upload, the only solution is to start the process once again." The same page asks you to make sure the computer isn't in standby while uploading. A 200 GB upload on a 50 Mbps line runs close to 9 hours at full speed. Keeping a laptop awake and online for 9 hours is the hard part.

**Two answers to one question.** The pricing page lists Pro with "Unlimited transfer size". The help center says "The size limit is 250 GB per transfer with Smash Pro, and 1 TB per transfer with Smash Team." We can't tell you which one is current. If you plan to send more than 250 GB on Pro, ask Smash first. Free link expiry has the same problem, 7 days on the pricing page and 14 on [the expiry help page](https://fromsmash.com/help/articles/12952074-how-can-i-change-the-availability-period-of-my-transfer).

## Which one should you pick?

- **You send 50 GB or more and can't babysit the upload.** Tranzfer. If the tab closes or the browser crashes, pick the same files again and only the missing parts go up. Starter holds 300 GB live at once for $15 a month.
- **You want a desktop app** that survives a reboot: MASV, billed per GB, or Filemail at a flat price.
- **Your sends are a few gigabytes** and the recipient expects a familiar name: WeTransfer.
- **You already pay for Dropbox** and send under 100 GB: Dropbox Transfer.

Plug your own sizes into the [price calculator](/pricing) to see what a month costs on each.

## When is Smash still the better pick?

- You want the cheapest flat plan. Smash Pro is $10 a month. Tranzfer Starter is $15.
- Your transfers are under 2 GB. Smash Free sends them at full priority, for nothing.
- You want branded pages. Smash Pro adds your logo and background to the send, receive and download pages.
- You want links that last up to 30 days on a paid plan. Tranzfer links last 14 days at most.
- You want an app. Smash has [a Mac app and phone apps](https://fromsmash.com/essential/smash-for-mobile) on the App Store and Google Play. Tranzfer runs only in the browser.

Smash also takes [folders straight from the browser](https://fromsmash.com/help/articles/13230506-how-can-i-send-a-folder) without zipping, so that's not a reason to switch.
