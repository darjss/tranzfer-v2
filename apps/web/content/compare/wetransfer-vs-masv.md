We make Tranzfer, a third option, so we've kept it to the end. Everything about WeTransfer and MASV here comes from their own pages, checked 9 October 2026.

## How does each one charge?

**WeTransfer sells plans with caps.** Its [plan limits page](https://wetransfer.com/help-center/subscriptions/plan-limits) puts Free at 10 transfers or 3 GB in any rolling 30 days, and Starter at 10 transfers or 300 GB. Ultimate allows unlimited transfers of up to 1 TB each. On Free and Starter, a link stays up for [3 days at most](https://wetransfer.com/help-center/how-to/transfer-availability). The [pricing page](https://wetransfer.com/pricing) asks you to sign up or sign in before it shows a price, so we don't quote one.

**MASV bills by the gigabyte.** Its [pricing page](https://masv.io/pricing) gives 15 GB free every month, then $0.25 per GB. The charge applies when data leaves MASV's network, as a download or a push to connected storage. Files stay free for 5 days, then cost $0.07 per GB a month. Annual plans lower the rate: $58 a month for 250 GB, $110 for 500 GB, $215 for 1 TB and $410 for 2 TB, with a 12-month commitment, and [unused data doesn't roll over](https://help.massive.io/en/does-unused-data-for-my-masv-subscription-roll-over-to-the-next-month). A credit card is needed to sign up, free tier included.

## What does a month of sending cost?

Each row is a month of deliveries, each downloaded once, one live at a time. MASV is pay as you go. WeTransfer shows which plan the month needs, because its prices aren't public.

| A month of sending  | WeTransfer plan it needs         | MASV pay as you go | Tranzfer       |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------ | -------------- |
| 1 × 50 GB           | Starter                          | $8.75              | $15 on Starter |
| 3 × 100 GB (300 GB) | Starter, right at its 300 GB cap | $71.25             | $15 on Starter |
| 4 × 250 GB (1 TB)   | Ultimate                         | $246.25            | $15 on Starter |
| 6 × 500 GB (3 TB)   | Ultimate                         | $746.25            | $29 on Pro     |

Two things move these numbers. If two people download the same MASV package, more data leaves the network; MASV's pricing page bills that egress per GB but doesn't spell out the two-recipient case. And Tranzfer counts what's live at once, so the same months cost more on Tranzfer if your deliveries overlap. The [price calculator](/pricing) runs your own sizes and counts.

## Which one fits how you send?

- **A few gigabytes now and then, to people who know the name.** WeTransfer Free, if you stay under 3 GB and 10 transfers a month.
- **Rare big sends.** MASV. A 50 GB delivery in a quiet month costs $8.75, and you pay nothing in a month you don't send.
- **Big sends every week.** MASV's bill climbs with every gigabyte. WeTransfer Ultimate is a flat plan with a 1 TB per transfer cap by its help center. Tranzfer is flat too, and counts what's live at once.
- **Client upload portals, integrations, a desktop app.** MASV has all three. WeTransfer and Tranzfer don't have MASV's portals.
- **Links that stay up for weeks.** WeTransfer Ultimate keeps transfers up as long as you like. MASV charges storage after 5 days. Tranzfer links end after 14 days at most.

## Where does Tranzfer fit?

It's the third option for people who send hundreds of gigabytes from a browser and want a flat bill. Plans are $15, $29 or $69 a month for 300 GB, 1 TB or 3 TB live at once, and every plan resumes an upload after a dropped connection, a sleeping laptop or a closed tab. We don't have a desktop app, portals or permanent links. The row-by-row versions are [Tranzfer vs MASV](/vs/masv) and [Tranzfer vs WeTransfer](/vs/wetransfer).
